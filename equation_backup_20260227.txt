Complete WAR Equation

1. Aggregate game logs → season totals per player

── OFFENSE ──────────────────────────────────────────────
o_xG60  = (EV_ixG + PP_ixG + SH_ixG) / toi_min × 60

── DEFENSE ──────────────────────────────────────────────
pm60        = plusMinus / toi_min × 60
β           = OLS(pm60 ~ o_xG60) on qualified players
pm60_resid  = pm60 − (α + β·o_xG60)
team_resid  = TOI-weighted team mean(pm60_resid)  [qual. only]
pm60_resid  = pm60_resid − team_resid             [team-adjust]
league_resid = TOI-weighted league mean(pm60_resid)
d_adj_pm60  = pm60_resid − league_resid
pm_val60    = d_adj_pm60 × 0.36

── BLOCKS (optional) ─────────────────────────────────────
blocks60        = season_blocks / toi_min × 60
blocks60_pos    = blocks60 − position_mean(F|D)
blocks60_adj    = blocks60_pos − TOI-weighted team mean(blocks60_pos)
block_val60     = blocks60_adj × 0.04

── COMBINED D VALUE ──────────────────────────────────────
d_value60  = pm_val60 + block_val60

── REPLACEMENT LEVELS (p25 of qualified pool) ────────────
o_repl  = percentile(25, o_xG60)
d_repl  = percentile(25, d_value60)

── GAR ───────────────────────────────────────────────────
oGAR = (o_xG60   − o_repl) × toi_min / 60
dGAR = (d_value60 − d_repl) × toi_min / 60

── WAR ───────────────────────────────────────────────────
gpw   = 2 × avg_goals_per_team_per_game   (Pythagorean)
oWAR  = oGAR / gpw
dWAR  = dGAR / gpw    (signed: good defense > 0)
WAR   = oWAR + dWAR
war60 = WAR / toi_min × 60

Constant	Value	Source

defense_weight	0.36	Tuned vs team GD Spearman across 3 seasons
block_weight	0.04	xG prevented per adjusted block/60

goals_per_win	2 × avg_goals/team/game	Pythagorean, computed per season
Step 1 — Offensive rate (box model)

o_xG60 = (EV_ixG + PP_ixG + SH_ixG) / toi_min × 60
Each ixG is the sum of xG_quality or xG_non_quality for every shot the player personally took, from the play-by-play.

Step 2 — Defensive rate (xGA model)
a. Attribute team shot quality against by TOI share, per game:


player_xGA_game = team_xGA_game × (player_toi_game / team_skater_toi_game)
b. Annualise:


xGA60 = Σ(player_xGA_game) / toi_min × 60
c. Regress out offensive zone deployment (OLS on qualified players):


xGA60_resid = xGA60 − (α + β × ixG60)
β is fit fresh each season — typically around −0.3 to −0.5 (more offensive players face fewer shots against)

d. Team-quality adjustment (TOI-weighted mean residual per team):


xGA60_resid = xGA60_resid − team_xGA_resid
e. League-mean adjustment and scale (negate: fewer xGA = good):


d_adj_xGA60 = xGA60_resid − league_resid
d_value60   = −d_adj_xGA60 × 0.36
f. Add blocked shots (if available):


blocks60     = blocks / toi_min × 60
blocks60_pos = blocks60 − position_mean        (removes F/D baseline)
blocks60_adj = blocks60_pos − team_mean        (removes team shot-volume effect)
d_value60   += blocks60_adj × 0.04
Step 3 — Goals Above Replacement

oGAR = (o_xG60   − o_repl) × toi_min / 60
dGAR = (d_value60 − d_repl) × toi_min / 60
Where o_repl = 25th percentile of o_xG60 and d_repl = 25th percentile of d_value60, both among qualified players (≥50 min TOI).

Step 4 — Wins Above Replacement

oWAR = oGAR / goals_per_win
dWAR = dGAR / goals_per_win
WAR  = oWAR + dWAR
What each piece captures
Term	Measures
o_xG60	Individual shot quality and volume
xGA60	Team shot quality allowed while player is on ice (TOI-attributed)
β × ixG60	Removes: forwards in offensive zone face fewer shots automatically
team_xGA_resid	Removes: playing on a stingy team inflates every player's dWAR
league_resid	Centers the distribution around zero
× −0.36	Scales defensive signal to goal-equivalent units; negates so fewer xGA = positive
blocks60_adj	Individual blocked shots, stripped of position and team shot-volume effects

Step 1 — Aggregate to season totals

toi_min     = sum of per-game TOI (minutes)
GP          = count of games with TOI > 0
total_ixG   = sum(EV_ixG + PP_ixG + SH_ixG)  [replaced by coord xG when PBP available]
plusMinus   = sum of +/-
blocks      = sum of blocked shots (from separate blocks CSV)
Step 2 — Offensive rate

o_xG60 = total_ixG / toi_min × 60
Step 3 — Defensive value (4-step pipeline)
3a. Raw rate:


pm60 = plusMinus / toi_min × 60
3b. OLS residual — remove linear relationship between +/- and individual shot generation (so scorers aren't rewarded twice):


[α, β] = OLS fit of pm60 ~ o_xG60  (qualified players only, toi_min ≥ 50)
pm60_resid = pm60 − (α + β × o_xG60)
3c. Team-quality adjustment — subtract each team's TOI-weighted mean residual so players are compared to teammates, not cross-team quality:


team_resid  = TOI-weighted mean of pm60_resid per team (qual. players)
pm60_resid  = pm60_resid − team_resid
3d. League-mean adjustment — zero-centre the metric:


league_resid = TOI-weighted mean of team-adjusted pm60_resid (all qual. players)
d_adj_pm60   = pm60_resid − league_resid
3e. Scale:


d_value60 = d_adj_pm60 × defense_weight    (= d_adj_pm60 × 0.36)
Step 3b — Blocked shots (optional addend to d_value60)

blocks60          = blocks / toi_min × 60
blocks60_pos_adj  = blocks60 − pos_mean[F or D]          (removes F/D baseline gap)
blocks60_adj      = blocks60_pos_adj − team_mean          (removes team shot-volume effect)
block_val60       = blocks60_adj × block_weight           (= blocks60_adj × 0.04)

d_value60         = d_value60 + block_val60
Step 4 — Combined value rate

value60 = o_xG60 + d_value60
Step 5 — Replacement levels (computed separately per component)

o_repl = 25th percentile of o_xG60    among qualified players
d_repl = 25th percentile of d_value60 among qualified players
Step 6 — Goals Above Replacement

oGAR = (o_xG60   − o_repl) × toi_min / 60
dGAR = (d_value60 − d_repl) × toi_min / 60
GAR  = oGAR + dGAR
Step 7 — Wins Above Replacement

oWAR  = oGAR / GPW
dWAR  = dGAR / GPW
WAR   = oWAR + dWAR
war60 = WAR / toi_min × 60

PWHL WAR — Full Equation
Inputs
Input	Source	Notes
EV_ixG, PP_ixG, SH_ixG	hockey-statistics.com SharePoint CSV	Per-player per-game; summed to season total
Coord xG (replaces CSV ixG where matched)	pwhl.hockey-statistics.com/api/game/playbyplay/{id}	PWHL-native logistic model trained on shot coordinates + shot quality label
FA (Fenwick shots against)	Same PBP API, per-game skater summary	Team's shots against in each game, attributed to each skater by TOI share
blocks	lscluster.hockeytech.com	Season total blocked shots per player
toi_min	CSV	Total ice time in minutes
plusMinus	CSV	Used only as fallback when FA unavailable
GPW	constants.py	2023-24 = 4.569, 2024-25 = 5.049, 2025-26 = 4.508
Step 1 — Offensive rate

o_xG60 = coord_xG_season / toi_min × 60
coord_xG is the PWHL-native model's predicted total expected goals for that player. Falls back to EV_ixG + PP_ixG + SH_ixG for the ~5% of players unmatched in the PBP.

Step 2 — Defensive rate (FA60)
2a. Raw FA60


FA60 = FA / toi_min × 60
where FA = Σ over games of (team_shots_against_game × player_toi_game / team_skater_toi_game)

2b. Unit-variance normalisation (among qualified players, toi_min ≥ 50)


FA60 = FA60 / std(FA60)
2c. OLS residual (remove offensive-zone deployment bias)


β, α  = OLS of FA60 ~ o_xG60   (qualified players only)
FA60_resid = FA60 − (α + β × o_xG60)
2d. League-mean centering (team_adjust = False — no team subtraction)


league_resid = TOI-weighted mean of FA60_resid   (qualified players)
d_adj_FA60   = FA60_resid − league_resid
2e. Scale and sign-flip (fewer shots against = positive value)


d_val60_FA = −d_adj_FA60 × 0.79
Step 3 — Blocked shots (optional)

blocks60         = blocks / toi_min × 60
blocks60_pos_adj = blocks60 − pos_mean[position]       (removes F/D baseline; D blocks ~2.5× more)
blocks60_adj     = blocks60_pos_adj − team_mean         (removes team shot-volume effect)
block_val60      = blocks60_adj × 0.04
Step 4 — Combined defensive value

d_value60 = d_val60_FA + block_val60
Step 5 — Replacement levels (p25 of qualified players)

o_repl = percentile(o_xG60,   25)
d_repl = percentile(d_value60, 25)
Step 6 — Goals Above Replacement

oGAR = (o_xG60   − o_repl) × toi_min / 60
dGAR = (d_value60 − d_repl) × toi_min / 60
Step 7 — Wins Above Replacement

GPW  = 2 × avg_goals_per_team_per_game   (Pythagorean)

oWAR = oGAR / GPW
dWAR = dGAR / GPW      (signed: negative = below replacement)
WAR  = oWAR + dWAR
Constants
Parameter	Value	Derivation
defense_weight	0.79	Exp-3 sweep (0.01–2.0), Spearman r=0.602 (p=0.014) vs team goal differential
block_weight	0.04	Approximate xG prevented per position+team-adjusted block/60
replacement_pct	25	p25 = replacement level
min_toi	50 min (full seasons), 25 min (2025-26 partial)	Data-gen floor; chart displays 2× that
